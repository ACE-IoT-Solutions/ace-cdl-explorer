# 2024 outdoor-air pollution sequence traceability

Source: [ASHRAE Guideline 36-2021 Addendum a, approved August 30, 2024](https://www.ashrae.org/file%20library/technical%20resources/standards%20and%20guidelines/standards%20addenda/g36_2021_a_20240830.pdf). The addendum supplies the 2024 pollution and economizer changes. The pinned OpenBuildingControl models used elsewhere in this project predate it. This matrix records the implementation and the evidence that is still needed; it is not a conformance declaration.

## Ownership boundary

The Rust building harness supplies weather, raw air-quality readings and availability, operator position, physical fan proof, measured airflow, and physical damper feedback. The graph produces pollution state, alarms, economizer permission, minimum-air target, and damper commands. `g36_control_contract` requires building actuator and mode outputs to have CDL block drivers; the physics does not infer a pollution state or choose an economizer position.

## Evidence needed to close this scope

1. A 2024 requirement-derived time-series suite that covers the transition cases listed above and exercises the **shipped composite** in both Rust executors. Pin its source clauses and expected transition times in the fixture.
2. A physical common-damper/relief configuration with design airflow, actuator and relief characteristics, and measured feedback. Verify mass flow and the minimum outdoor-air target across the staged transition and release.
3. A chosen project configuration and operator schedule: installed pollutant sensors, filter ratings and PM2.5 limits per AHU, local daily reminder time, and high-limit device for the applicable climate and design.
